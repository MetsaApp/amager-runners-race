03 / Practical

Know before you run

P
Parking
Free parking at Amager Strandpark north and south lots. Limited spaces — we strongly recommend the Metro (Øresund st., 4 min walk).
B
Bag drop
Staffed bag drop from 17:30 until 21:30 at Helgoland Badeanstalt. Bring your bib number — no claim tickets needed.
H
Hydration
One water station at the start/finish area. 10K runners pass it once when they cross the line at the end of the first loop. Cups compostable.
M
Medical
Medics at start, finish and at the 5K mark of the 10K. Emergency number printed on the back of your bib.
C
Chip timing
Disposable chip on the back of your bib — no return required. Live results at race.amagerrunners.dk from 18:40.
